About Fika med Hannah

Fika med Hannah is a cosy corner of the internet for Swedish learners who want lessons that feel like a chat over coffee rather than a classroom lecture. What’s your Swedish level?

From your very first ord to fluent conversation — here’s roughly what each CEFR level looks like.

  • A1

    Beginner. Greetings, basic phrases and simple everyday words.

  • A2

    Elementary. Short, simple conversations about familiar topics.

  • B1

    Intermediate. Handling everyday situations and sharing opinions.

  • B2

    Upper intermediate. Confident, detailed conversation on most topics.

  • C1

    Advanced. Fluent, flexible use of Swedish in work and daily life.

  • C2

    Mastery. Near-native understanding and expression.
